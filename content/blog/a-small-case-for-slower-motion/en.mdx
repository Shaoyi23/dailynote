A lot of interface motion is designed to get out of the way. Quick fades, short slides, a spring that resolves before you can name it. Speed feels professional, so we keep making things faster.

But speed is only one part of the job. A transition also has to explain where something came from, where it went, and what changed while it moved. If it finishes too early, the explanation is lost.

## match the motion to the thought

A menu opening can be quick. A layout change that rearranges the page deserves a little more time. The useful question is not how fast can this be, but how long does it take to understand what happened.

> The best motion is not the shortest. It is the one that leaves no gap between action and understanding.
